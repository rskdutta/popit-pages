FAQ

Who are the viewers?

A simulated panel: ten to twenty viewers with an age, a taste and a patience, most inside the audience you picked and a few deliberately outside it. They react second by second and write what they would say. The page always says they are simulated; real viewers replace them as they arrive.

Will it change my reel?

No. It reads a working copy and never touches your file. The coach names one change; you make it, upload the new cut, and the workbench compares the two.

How is this different from analytics?

Analytics tell you what happened after you posted. POPIT tells you what will happen at 0:04, before you post, and what to change.

Where do my reels go, and who can see them?

Into your own space on our servers, under your own id. Nobody else can list, read or play them. Delete a reel and every reading of it goes with it.

Is anything trained on my reels?

No. Nothing you upload trains any model, ours or anyone's.

How long does a reading take?

About ten to fifteen minutes for a short reel. You can watch it being read: the content first, then each viewer as they finish, then the coach.
