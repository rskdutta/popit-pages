03 · Coach my reel

One template. Four coaches. One change per version.

Every reel that sells follows the same shape: a hook that makes skipping feel like a loss, a promise, proof on screen, a payoff, and one clear ask. Coach my reel lays your reel over that template, scaled to its length, and tells you — stage by stage — what is met, what is partly there and what is missing.

The hook coach

The first three seconds are a bet against the scroll. Movement before words, a reason to stay, no logo card.

The editor

Pace and rhythm. Cuts where attention dips, captions that stay long enough to read, a message that holds with the sound off.

The strategist

One idea landed. The proof matches the claim. The ask matches the offer.

The story shaper

Which story your reel is actually telling, and which part of that story is thin.

What the coach hands you

  • A stage strip — Hook to ask, each stage marked met, partly or missing.
  • The counsel — Plain words per stage, with the seconds to watch.
  • A checklist — What to keep and what to cut.
  • Next version, change one thing — One edit and the result it should move, so you can measure it: “Open on the hands, not the logo. Hypothesis: more viewers past 0:03.”
