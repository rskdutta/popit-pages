See your reel the way the feed sees it

Before a viewer feels anything, the platform has already read your reel: its cuts, its captions, its spoken words, when the product appears, whether it works with the sound off. Content engagement gives you that reading, beat by beat, in plain language.

What you get

  • Sound-off test — Most feeds start muted. You learn whether the message survives on pictures and captions alone.
  • The storyline — Your reel as chapters: who is on screen, where, with what, and whether each chapter earns the next.
  • Attention budget — How many seconds go to the hook, the demonstration, the offer — and where the budget is wasted.
  • Fixes with time stamps — “Trim 0:04–0:08”, “Bring the product forward to 0:02”: concrete edits, each tied to a moment you can watch.
