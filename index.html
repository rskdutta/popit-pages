How a reel moves through PoP It

Upload once. Three readings come back on the same timeline, in plain language, with the seconds they refer to.

1

Upload the reel

Drop the file. Add your category, audience and goal. That is the whole brief.

2

A panel watches it

Viewers in and outside your audience watch, react at the moment it happens and write what they would say.

3

The content is read

Every cut, caption, spoken line and product moment is placed on the timeline and judged for clarity with the sound on and off.

4

The coach speaks

Hook, promise, proof, payoff and call to action — each stage met, partly met or missing, with the fix in the coach’s words.

One timeline. Three readings.

The panel’s reactions, the content read and the coach’s stages all sit on the same seconds, so the fix is never “somewhere in the middle” — it is at 0:04.
